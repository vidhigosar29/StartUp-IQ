Beyond central schemes

India's startup incubators, mapped

Technology Business Incubators (TBIs), Atal Incubation Centres (AICs), academic incubators and startup hubs across India — the on-ground infrastructure behind the schemes. Most are supported by the Department of Science & Technology (DST-NIDHI), the Atal Innovation Mission (AIM), MeitY or state governments. Explore them on the map, browse state-wise, or search by name, host institution, city or sector.
